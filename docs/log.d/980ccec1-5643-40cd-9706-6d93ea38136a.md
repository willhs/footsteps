## [2026-11-22] feature | Ice sheets at their dated extent (?ice=1)

Task 0013 (`docs/tasks/0013-ice-sheets-dated-extent/`), research 0014 rank 2. Source: PaleoMIST 1.0 (Gowan et al. 2021, PANGAEA
doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.905800, CC BY 4.0), read by HTTP range from its 3.7 GB zip (only the margin files, CRC-32 checked, sha256 pinned in
`footstep-generator/ice_sheets_sources.json`). Its Eurasian margins follow DATED-1's most-credible slices; DATED-1 and Dalton 2020 are not
used separately. `ice_sheets.py` rasterises Eurasia, North America and Patagonia to one 2.5′ mask per 2.5 ka epoch, 0-80 ka (99 patches,
353 KB; one epoch ~12 KB) with `public/ice/ice_sheets.json`. A year shows the nearest epoch, never interpolated. Reproduced against
PaleoMIST's own 1° thickness grid (no node ours-only, 82-89% agree) and DATED-1's 5.5 million km² LGM area (ours 5.59 at 20 ka).
Behind `?ice=1` (off by default): a white overlay with a blue-grey edge, an ice surface in the close-up (no height), ice patches as extra
exclusions in the site ranker (cells all ice counted `unresolved`, totals kept), a hover row "Ice sheet (PaleoMIST 1.0, 22.5 ka)" and a
note. Antarctica left out. See `docs/ops/ice-sheets.md`.
